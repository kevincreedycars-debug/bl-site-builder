# The raw material, by stage

Nothing is in here yet. The folders say where each stage's output goes, so a
draft cannot be written into the same place as the research behind it.

    briefs/     one idea, one angle, one source set. Written before any prose.
    ledgers/    the claim ledger for one brief: every fact in the draft, the
                source it came from, and the date that source was read.
    drafts/     the prose. May only use facts that exist in the ledger.

The rule that makes the three worth separating: a draft with no ledger is never
approved. A claim with no source line is a blocker, not a creative choice. The
account of why is in `STRATEGY.md` and in `docs/strategy/PROJECT_BRIEF.md`,
part 6.

Nothing here is served to the browser, and nothing here may hold a key.
